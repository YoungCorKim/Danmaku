Vision for Danmaku (Deliverable 1)

This text describes the planned game for the semester deliverable.

High-level vision:
- A top-down bullet-hell shooter consisting of 4 phases: regular waves, mid-boss, more waves, final boss.
- Gameplay lasts ~2-3 minutes for the demo. Player has lives and score.

Required features for Deliverable 1 (implemented or scaffolded):
1. Player
  - 8-direction movement via arrow keys or WASD
  - Normal and Slow mode (hold Shift for slow precision)
  - Small hitbox used for precise dodging

2. Enemies and bosses
  - At least two grunt enemy types (A and B) and two bosses (mid and final).
  - Enemies can move and fire bullets; bosses have staged attacks.

3. Systems
  - Bullet pooling via a BulletFactory to support many simultaneous bullets without GC spikes.
  - EnemyFactory for spawning and reusing enemy objects.
  - Simple stage flow manager (to be implemented) that sequences waves and boss fights.

Implementation notes / next steps:
- Replace placeholder textures (`player`, `bullet`, `enemyA`, `enemyB`, `midboss`, `finalboss`) with art in `Content/Content.mgcb` and rebuild content.
- Implement enemy patterns and boss stages (mid boss and final boss with at least two distinct final boss stages).
- Add collision detection, health, scoring, and UI.
- Polish: audio, visual effects, and packaging.
